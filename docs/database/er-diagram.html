What each role can reach

Every business table carries tenant_id, and the database itself (row level security) checks the role on every query. A rep's team is never stored twice: it comes from users.manager_id.

Super admin

users.tenant_id IS NULL

Runs the platform: plans, limits, default cues, tenants. Sees no meetings, deals or documents. Gets seat and meeting counts only, through the tenant_usage view.

Client admin

tenant_id = own tenant

Everything inside their company: users, integrations, documents, knowledge, billing, every lead and call.

Manager

rep_id / owner_id IN (users WHERE manager_id = me)

Their assigned reps' leads, calls, lessons and mock meetings. Can build lessons for a rep and leave feedback.

Sales rep

rep_id / owner_id = me

Their own leads, calls, lessons, mock meetings and chats. Cannot see colleagues' data.

Design choices

One company boundary

tenant_id sits on every business table, so one query filter (and one database policy) keeps companies apart. Only super admins have no tenant.

A call without a lead

meetings.lead_id may be empty. Linking an orphan call to a new lead only sets that column. Leads typed in by hand use source = draft and leave the CRM columns empty.

Cues: defaults and overrides

Platform defaults have no tenant_id. A company's replacement points back through overrides_cue_id. Calls score against the cue row that was in force, so history stays true.

Lessons store the cues, not the script

A lesson is a title plus its cue set (practice_lesson_cues) and where it came from. The conversation is rebuilt from templates, so improving a template improves every lesson.

Calculated, not stored

A manager's team, a lead's call count and days in stage, a cue average, a tenant's seats and meetings used, and a source's citation count are views (lead_stats, tenant_usage, knowledge_usage). Storing them invites numbers that disagree.

A call has two sources, one meeting

Salesforce knows who and when (crm_activities); the Drive folder holds the audio (media_files). meetings joins them, and sits in awaiting_audio until the file arrives. Neither side can import the same call twice.

Companies cannot be mixed up

Links between company tables are composite keys on (tenant_id, id). A meeting cannot point at another company's rep, deal or file, even if the app has a bug.

Rules live in the database

One manager per rep, in the same company; the plan's seat limit; buyer-only objections; credits that cannot go negative or be charged twice. Each is a constraint or trigger, and tests/verify.sql proves them.

Ledgers only grow

credit_transactions, audit_log and platform_audit_log refuse UPDATE and DELETE. Every manager change is also kept in manager_assignments.

Per-deal coaching

AI-built lessons for a deal sit in coaching_programs and coaching_lessons. Lessons a user builds from cues stay in practice_lessons. Attempts and manager feedback attach to either.

Not in this map

The audio and document files themselves (object storage, referenced by storage_key), the speech-to-text and analysis workers, the login provider, and semantic search vectors (add pgvector to knowledge_chunks when needed).
